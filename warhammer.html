Grimdark Racing

Kart Racer Unreal Engine 5 Team Project 2nd Year Final

Grimdark Racing is a kart racer inspired by the Warhammer 40,000 universe, developed as the final project of my second year at university. The game pits iconic Warhammer factions against each other on a custom race track set in the grim darkness of the far future.

Working within a team, I focused on level design and overall game design direction — crafting the track layout, designing the weapon pickups, and ensuring each vehicle felt distinct both visually and mechanically.

The project required balancing deep Warhammer lore with accessible kart-racing gameplay, making design decisions that honoured the source material while remaining fun for players unfamiliar with the universe.
